### 🛠️ What I'm building

**🫀 Nadi: Indonesian-language AI news portal** · [nadi.veronicalabs.id](https://nadi.veronicalabs.id)
- An agent-assisted newsroom: bots score news sources and draft articles, then a human editor reviews and publishes every piece.
- Transparent AI use: every AI-assisted article is labeled on its page.
- 20+ articles published plus a weekly newsletter (as of October 2026).

**More from Veronica Labs**
- 🧾 **[CekPajak UMKM](https://cekpajakumkm.veronicalabs.id)**: checks whether an MSME qualifies for Indonesia's 0.5% final income tax, in two minutes.
- 📊 **[Olah Data Statistika](https://ujiasumsi.my.id)**: six classical assumption tests in the browser, with explanations in Bahasa Indonesia and Word reports.
- 🎓 **Kelas Agentic AI**: a beginner class on how AI agents work and how to use them, taught live.
- 🤖 **AI agents for UMKM**: a managed AI assistant for each small business *(in development)*.

**Side projects**
- 📬 **Email triage agent**: a Telegram bot that triages multiple Gmail inboxes with Claude. It sends real-time alerts, daily digests, and one-tap actions (archive, label, reply), and it is self-hosted with Docker.
- 👁️ **VeroEye**: a Telegram bot built on Hermes Agent that turns a second-hand webcam into a smart home camera. It runs on motionEye and reports motion, and because it is LLM-based, it can tell whether it sees a person or something else and describe the condition of the room.
- 🏠 **Home lab**: mini PCs running self-hosted AI agents (Hermes Agent by Nous Research), a Jellyfin media server, a self-hosted archive for personal documents, and secure remote access. This is my playground for learning infrastructure the hands-on way.
